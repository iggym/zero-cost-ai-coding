Stack Trace Autopsy

Paste a production stack trace and get an instant human-readable summary: the error, the exact user-code line that failed, and ready-to-click Google + Stack Overflow search links. Runs entirely in your browser — no API, no key.

limit: ∞ — pure client-side parsing, no network calls

What it replaces

Pasting the same trace into ChatGPT, asking a coworker in Slack, or paying for observability platforms just to decode an error. It doesn't fix your bug — it just tells you where the body is.

How to use

Paste any JavaScript/Node, Python, Java, Go, Ruby, or PHP stack trace. Click Autopsy. Use the search links to research, or copy the markdown summary to drop into a bug ticket.

What breaks

      
Only supports the major formats listed above — weird or heavily customized error formats won't parse correctly. Extremely long traces (10k+ lines) may lag a bit in the browser. This is pattern matching, not AI — if your trace is mangled or missing symbols, the parser will skip those frames rather than invent data.
