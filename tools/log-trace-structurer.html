How to use it

  • Paste raw console logs, multi-line stack traces, or previously exported JSON.
  • Click Structure Logs to auto-detect log levels, ISO timestamps, and stack traces.
  • Export or copy the normalized array using the standardized `zcac` schema.

What breaks

Extremely large log files (>100MB) may freeze your browser tab during regex operations. Process logs in smaller batches for optimal speed.
