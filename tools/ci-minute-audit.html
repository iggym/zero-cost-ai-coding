What breaks

  • At the cap: with no payment method on file, runs are blocked once the 2,000 minutes are gone — jobs don't queue, they refuse to start. With a card on file you're billed per minute instead.
  • Cache isn't free space: 10 GB per repository, and entries unused for 7 days are evicted. A monorepo caching target/ and node_modules will thrash and you'll pay the download cost with none of the hit rate.
  • cancel-in-progress is dangerous on deploys. Group it by workflow+ref for CI only. Cancelling a half-finished release job is a worse day than 40 wasted minutes.
  • The parser is a subset. It reads block mappings, block/flow sequences and anchors-free YAML. Reusable workflows (uses: at job level), &anchors, and multi-document files are reported as unparsed rather than guessed at.
  • Estimates are your assumptions × structure. This tool does not read your run history, so it can't know real job durations. Feed it your actual average from the Actions tab.
