Safe-Paste PII Firewall

Paste your raw logs or code. Watch PII vanish before it hits the clipboard.

Developers constantly paste production logs into AI tools, accidentally leaking emails, AWS keys, and IPs. Enterprise Data Loss Prevention (DLP) tools solve this, but they require expensive contracts or complex backend setups. This tool runs entirely in your browser.

Instructions

  1. For chatbots: Pick your model tab, click Copy, paste into ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini, replace {{variables}}, send.
