OCR Extractor

Browser-Native OCR

Extract text from images directly in your browser using Tesseract.js WASM. 100% free, no API keys, no upload limits.

Share on X

What it replaces

AWS Textract ($1.50 per 1,000 pages) or Google Cloud Vision API for simple OCR tasks. You don't need a backend or a credit card to pull text out of a screenshot.

How to use it

  1. Drop an image, click to select, or paste directly from clipboard (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V).
  2. Wait for the WASM engine to load and process (runs locally on your machine).
  3. Copy the extracted text or download as structured JSON.

What breaks

Handwriting or heavily stylized fonts might not be recognized accurately. Large images are downscaled to 1000px max to prevent your browser tab from freezing during processing.
