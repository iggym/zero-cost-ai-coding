AI Error Explainer

Paste a stack trace or error message to get a plain-English explanation and suggested fixes using Gemini 1.5 Flash. Free up to 1,500 requests/day.

Share on X

What it replaces

Paid developer tools with built-in AI analysis, or copy-pasting errors into ChatGPT and losing context. Tools like Sentry offer AI autofix as part of their paid Team/Enterprise plans.

How to use it

  1. Get a free API key from Google AI Studio (no credit card required).
  2. Paste your key below. It is stored in sessionStorage only and is wiped when you close this tab.
  3. Paste your error or stack trace and click "Explain Error".

What breaks

If you send more than 15 requests in a minute, Google will rate-limit you for 60 seconds. Very long stack traces may be truncated by the model's context window.
