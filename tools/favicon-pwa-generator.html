What it replaces

Paid or ad-heavy favicon generators that require uploading your assets to their servers, or manually resizing images in Photoshop and hand-writing the manifest.

How to use it

1. Select a PNG, JPG, or SVG source image (square works best).
2. Click "Generate Assets".
3. Download the ZIP containing favicon.ico, PNGs, and site.webmanifest.
4. Copy the HTML snippet into your <head>.

What breaks

Extremely large source images (>5000x5000px) might crash the browser tab due to memory limits. Keep your source image reasonably sized (e.g., 512x512 or 1024x1024).
