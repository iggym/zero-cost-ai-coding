01 · why this exists

Chunking is the step people skip — then retrieval suffers.

Embeddings get all the attention, but chunk size and overlap decide whether a RAG system actually surfaces the right passage. A chunk too small loses context; too large dilutes the signal; too much overlap wastes storage and creates near-duplicate vectors. The defaults in popular frameworks are reasonable starting points, not answers — and they're invisible until something breaks.
